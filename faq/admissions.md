# Admissions FAQ – BVPS Kalayat

### Q1. For which classes are admissions open?
A: Admissions open for Classes 1 to 12 as per seats available.

### Q2. What is the age criteria for Class 1?
A: As per Haryana Board (BSEH) norms. Contact school office for current session.

### Q3. How to apply for admission?
A: Fill [Online Application Form](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/application) or collect form from school office.

### Q4. What documents are required?
A: Birth Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Previous Report Card, Transfer Certificate (if coming from another school), Passport Size Photos (3–4), Address Proof.

### Q5. Is registration chargeable?
A: Please refer [Admissions](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/admissions) page or contact office.

### Q6. When will admission test/interview be conducted?
A: Shortlisted candidates will be informed via call/website notice.

### Q7. Can I pay fees online?
A: For fee related queries, visit [Fee Structure](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/fee-structure) or contact school office.
