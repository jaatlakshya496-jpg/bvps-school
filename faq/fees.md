# Fee Structure FAQ – BVPS Kalayat

### Q1. Where can I see complete fee structure?
A: [Fee Structure 2026–27](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/fee-structure)

### Q2. Are fees same for all classes?
A: No, fees vary by class. Check class-wise table on fee page.

### Q3. What is included in fees?
A: Admission fee, monthly fee, annual fund etc as shown. Contact office for clarity.

### Q4. When is fee due?
A: Monthly/quarterly as per school notice. Check with office.

### Q5. Is there transport fee?
A: Yes, if availing transport. Transport details available at office.
