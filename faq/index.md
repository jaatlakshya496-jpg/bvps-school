# BVPS Kalayat – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Bal Vikas Public School (BVPS), Railway Road, Kalayat, Kaithal, Haryana – established 2004, Classes 1 to 12 (HBSE/BSEH).

## General Questions

### Q1. What is the full name and address of BVPS?
A: Bal Vikas Public School, Railway Road, Kalayat, Distt. Kaithal, Haryana – 136117.

### Q2. Which board is BVPS affiliated to?
A: BVPS is affiliated to Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH/HBSE).

### Q3. Since when is BVPS running?
A: The school was established in 2004.

### Q4. Which classes does BVPS offer?
A: Classes 1 to 12 (co-educational).

### Q5. What is the school code?
A: School Code – 11498.

## Admissions

### Q6. When are admissions open?
A: Admissions are generally open for Classes 1–12. Please check [Admissions](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/admissions) page or contact school office.

### Q7. What documents are required for admission?
A: Birth certificate, Aadhaar card, previous class report card, transfer certificate (if applicable), passport size photos, residence proof etc. See [Admissions](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/admissions) for full list.

### Q8. How can I apply?
A: Apply online via [Apply for Admission](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/application) or visit school office.

## Fees

### Q9. Where can I see fee structure?
A: Check [Fee Structure](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/fee-structure) page (Classes 1–12, 2026–27).

### Q10. Are there any extra charges?
A: Fee details are shown class-wise on fee structure page. For exact breakup, contact school office.

## Academics & Streams

### Q11. Which streams are available in Senior Secondary?
A: Medical, Non-Medical and Commerce. See [Streams](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/streams).

### Q12. What facilities are available?
A: Smart classrooms, library, computer lab, science labs, playground, sports, transport etc. See [Facilities](https://bvps-school.vercel.app/facilities).
